Affiliate Disclosure

Transparency in our tool recommendations, hardware links, and lumber resource guides.

1. FTC Disclosure Guidelines

In compliance with Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines, please assume that certain links pointing to woodworking tools, pocket hole jigs, hardware, finishes, and safety gear on AlphaWoodworkingPlan are affiliate links.

2. What Is an Affiliate Link?

If you click on an affiliate link and make a purchase from the merchant (such as Amazon Services LLC, Home Depot, Rockler Woodworking and Hardware, or Woodcraft), AlphaWoodworkingPlan may receive a small commission from the merchant at zero additional cost to you. The price you pay remains exactly the same whether you use our link or go directly to the merchant's website.

3. Our Editorial Independence

We do not accept paid placements to recommend low-quality tools. Every tool, fastener, and finish mentioned in our plans—from Kreg pocket-hole screws to Titebond III waterproof wood glue—is recommended because it genuinely works in real workshop conditions. Our primary goal is providing accurate, actionable woodworking blueprints for our readers.
