Our Mission

About AlphaWoodworkingPlan

Dedicated to making woodworking honest, accessible, and deeply satisfying for everyday homeowners, hobbyists, and weekend crafters across America.

Why We Started AlphaWoodworkingPlan

Too many online woodworking plans are either ridiculously complicated CAD drawings designed for commercial CNC machines, or vague Pinterest graphics that leave out critical lumber cut dimensions, pocket hole depths, and fastener specs. You buy lumber, make your first three cuts, and realize the pieces don't fit together.

We created AlphaWoodworkingPlan to solve that frustration. Every blueprint, step-by-step tutorial, and cut list published on our platform is built on four core principles:

1. Realistic Tools & Accessible Lumber

You don't need a $4,000 cast-iron cabinet saw or an industrial jointer. Our plans are engineered around standard tools available at any local home center—miter saws, circular saws, pocket-hole jigs, cordless drills, and random orbital sanders—using off-the-shelf 2x4s, cedar, and select pine boards.

2. Honest US Customary Measurements

We clearly distinguish between nominal dimensions (what the label at Home Depot or Lowe's says) and actual dimensions (what the board actually measures with your tape). Our cut lists account for actual board thicknesses, kerf loss from saw blades, and wood movement.

3. Transparent Budget Estimates

Lumber prices fluctuate by region and season. We provide honest, realistic USD price bands for every build and specify whether you're purchasing construction pine, knotty cedar, or premium hardwood.

4. Non-Negotiable Workshop Safety

Woodworking should bring joy, not injuries. We highlight essential safety gear, proper push-stick techniques, dust protection, and safe tool operation in every single build guide.
