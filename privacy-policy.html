Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026. How AlphaWoodworkingPlan respects and protects your digital privacy.

1. Overview

AlphaWoodworkingPlan (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use that information, and how we keep it secure.

2. Information We Collect

Voluntary Information: When you subscribe to our email newsletter or submit a contact message, you voluntarily provide your name and email address. We use this information solely to deliver the newsletter updates you requested or respond to your inquiry.

Automated Analytics Data: Like most publications, we collect non-identifiable usage metrics such as browser type, operating system, pages visited, referring URLs, and time spent on pages to improve our woodworking content and site navigation speed.

You may unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by clicking the “Unsubscribe” link in any email footer. If you wish to request full deletion of any stored contact data, email us directly at privacy@alphawoodworkingplan.com.
